ort climate and energy solutions without becoming a silver bullet.[1]

The more useful question is therefore not “Does AI have an environmental footprint?” Every material technology does. It is: What value is created per unit of environmental burden, which alternatives were available, who receives the value, who bears the local cost, and what rules cause efficiency gains to become real reductions rather than permission to consume without limit?

AI's environmental future will not be decided by the prompt alone. It will be decided by the defaults, grids, cooling systems, procurement contracts, disclosure rules, business models, and collective choices hidden behind it.
